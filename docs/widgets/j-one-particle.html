J · One-particle convergence

This one was planned and is not built. Two reasons, and both of them are worth more to a reader than a widget would have been.

Why there is no widget here

1. The figures it would rebuild have no generating code

Figures S2–S6 of the Supplemental Material — the wavelet-route \(L^2\) error at \(c=0\), the momentum-cutoff \(L^2\) error at \(c=\tfrac12,1\), and the off-diagonal Hilbert–Schmidt error — arrive as PDFs produced in 2021. There is no script in this repository that regenerates them.

Until there is, this repository is not reproducible end to end, and a widget that looked like those figures would be a new computation dressed up as a reproduction. The honest move is to say so, leave the gap visible, and close it by writing the generator and comparing the new curves against the 2021 PDFs before replacing them — because if they differ, that is a finding and it belongs in the supplement, not in a footnote.

Tracked in Status → Open. The one number the site actually uses from these figures is the \(\approx0.4\) of the near-term point, and widget G marks it as taken on trust.

2. The mathematics belongs to the companion repository

The quantities plotted in Figs. S2–S6 are one-particle objects: operator norms and Hilbert–Schmidt norms of the difference between a Koo–Saleur approximant and a Virasoro generator, along two renormalization routes, as functions of the Sobolev regularity of a Daubechies scaling function. That is the subject of the companion paper, and its own site already computes exactly these — momentum_cutoff_opnorm, momentum_cutoff_hs, wavelet_route_opnorm, sobolev_sigma — live in the browser, from the filter taps.

Two independent implementations of the same generator is how two projects drift apart without either noticing. So this site links across rather than duplicating:

the companion site, which computes these quantities live, and the sources at github.com/alexander-stottmeister/lattice_cft — Conformal field theory from lattice fermions: the regularity thresholds, the sharp one-particle rates, the two routes, the energy bounds, the multiplicity criterion. (It is also the target of the citation [v5] throughout this site, whose manuscript is served at free_fermion_cft_v5.pdf.)

What this site does compute from the same expansion

The mechanism behind all of Figs. S2–S6 is one expansion, and that one is recomputed here, because it is the hinge of the error budget rather than a result of the companion paper: widget C draws \(\varepsilon_N^{-1}\sin(\varepsilon_Nx)-x=-\tfrac16\varepsilon_N^2x^3+\cdots\) and measures its decay rate as \(4.000\) per step of \(N\). What it does not do is evaluate the weighted operator norms of Lemma S1 or the Hilbert–Schmidt sums of Lemma S2.

What this page does not show. Anything at all. There is no computation on it, and therefore no check badge: a badge here would be decoration.
