C · The Koo–Saleur symbol

Every rate on this site descends from one Taylor expansion. The lattice generator’s one-particle symbol is \(\varepsilon_N^{-1}\sin(\varepsilon_N x)\) where the continuum generator’s is \(x\), with \(x=l\mp\tfrac k2\); the difference is \(-\varepsilon_N^2x^3/6+\cdots\), and that \(\varepsilon^2=4^{-N}\) at \(L=1\) is the whole rate.

Lattice symbol against continuum symbol

Statement: Lemma S1, “Source and mechanism”, and Lemma S2 for the off-diagonal strip. The Koo–Saleur ansatz itself is in Definitions.

What this does not show. This is the one-particle symbol of the diagonal channel, in the phase-free normalization of the numerics script. It is not the full \(2\times2\) Nambu matrix \(\ell^{(N)}_k(l',l)\) of the Letter, and it says nothing about the second-quantization lift (Lemma S3) or about the Sobolev weight \(\lVert\xi\rVert_{h^{1+\delta_s}}\) that turns a pointwise bound into an operator bound.

Reading the curves

The matrix elements of the scale-matched operator \(\widetilde\ell^{(N)}_{\pm,k}\) in the plane-wave basis are explicit, and the difference to \(\ell_{\pm,k}\) is a finite sum of

$$\varepsilon_N^{-1}\sin\bigl(\varepsilon_N(l\mp\tfrac k2)\bigr)-\bigl(l\mp\tfrac k2\bigr) \;=\;-\tfrac16\varepsilon_N^{2}\bigl(l\mp\tfrac k2\bigr)^{3}+O\bigl(\varepsilon_N^{4}x^{5}\bigr).$$
  • Two powers of \(\varepsilon_N\), three powers of \(x\). That is why Lemma S1 carries the weight \(h^{1+\delta_s}\): the deviation grows cubically in the momentum, so a bound uniform over the band needs three derivatives’ worth of decay on the vector, of which regularity of the smearing supplies \(1+\delta_s\). Two-regular smearing, \(K\ge5\), gives \(\delta_s=2\) and saturates the rate.
  • The rate is sharp. Not an artefact of the estimate: Lemma S1 says so explicitly. There is no route to \(\varepsilon_N^3\) along the momentum-cutoff group.
  • The order \(k\) shifts the argument, it does not change the shape. The whole \(k\)-dependence of the diagonal channel is the recentring \(x=l\mp k/2\); the genuinely \(k\)-dependent cost is the off-diagonal strip \(0<|l|<|k|\) of Lemma S2, which has \(2(|k|-1)\) points and is empty for the Möbius generators \(k=0,\pm1\).
