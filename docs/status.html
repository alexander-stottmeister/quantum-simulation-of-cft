Status

What is settled, what is assembled here, what rests on a computation, and what is open. Nothing on this site has been refereed; read every statement as a claim under verification. The manuscript is between submissions and the revision is recent.

Established, and where

These are the inputs the error analysis consumes. All are cited; most are cited to the revised and expanded version of the companion paper, written [v5, …], which is a preprint, not a refereed article. The published article is Commun. Math. Phys. 398, 219 (2023).

  • Strong, hence strong-resolvent, convergence of the Koo–Saleur approximants \(L^{(N)}_k\to L_k\) and uniform-on-compact-time convergence of the correlators. [CMP 398, Thms. 4.6, 4.11, 4.16, 6.1, 6.3]; [v5, Thms. 4.16, 4.25, 6.1, 6.5]
  • A sharp one-particle rate \(\varepsilon_N^{\min\{\delta_s,2\}}\) for all orders \(k\) along the momentum-cutoff renormalization group. [v5, Lem. 4.15, Thm. 4.16]
  • The wavelet-route rates: \(\varepsilon_N^2\) for \(k=0\), only \(\varepsilon_N\) for \(k\neq0\), with the quadratic rate restored by a re-centred comparison. [v5, Rmks. 4.8, 4.32, 3.5]
  • An explicit lattice-vacuum rate. [v5, Prop. 6.3]
  • \(N\)-uniform linear energy bounds for the lattice generators. [v5, Lem. 4.17, Prop. 6.6]
  • Energy growth and Duhamel estimates with explicit time dependence. [v5, Lems. 4.12, 4.19]
  • For the chiral Hamiltonian evolution \(k=0\) along the momentum-cutoff route, an explicit correlator-level bound with all constants, and the resulting qubit budget. [v5, Prop. 6.3, Rmk. 6.4]

Assembled here, and therefore the thing to attack

What the Supplemental Material adds on its own account is the propagation of the one-particle rate to the full \(m\)-point correlator for the local conformal transformations, \(k\neq0\): through second quantization, the lattice vacuum, the unbounded Heisenberg dynamics on \([0,T]\), and the multi-point telescoping. The constant is left in factored form.

Three things follow, and they should be read exactly as written:

  • What the assembly establishes is the rate \(\varepsilon_N^2\) — equivalently the exponent \(\tfrac12\) in \(n(\delta,T)\sim C_T\delta^{-1/2}\) — together with the explicit \(T\)- and \(m\)-dependence.
  • The numerical value of the prefactor \(C_0\) for \(k\neq0\) is not computed. Nothing on this site turns it into a number, and the error-budget widget draws that branch as a shape with the prefactor divided out, not as an error bar.
  • The strong-resolvent convergence carries no rate, and no rate is at any point extracted from it. The Trotter–Kato argument appears in the supplement only as a qualitative cross-check.

For \(k=0\), nothing is assembled: the bound is [v5, Prop. 6.3] with all constants.

Corrections the revision makes

The published companion article and the 2022 submitted version of this Letter each contain statements that do not survive. They are listed rather than quietly dropped.

In the published companion article

  • The published one-particle error estimates, [CMP 398, eqs. (204)–(210)], rest on a domination step that does not hold as stated. The published rate is established for the Hamiltonian \(k=0\) only; an \(N\)-uniform majorant replaces the step. [v5, Lem. 4.7, Rmks. 4.32–4.33; Lem. 3.14, Cor. 3.15]
  • The Möbius simplification [CMP 398, Rmk. 4.14] — that for \(k=0,\pm1\) the wavelet route alone suffices — does not follow from its proof, because the Hardy projections do not preserve the wavelet space. Only the weaker statement is used: the Hilbert–Schmidt part vanishes. [v5, Rmk. 4.22]
  • The \(N\)-uniform energy bound underlying the Virasoro correlation functions had been asserted in the proof of [CMP 398, Thm. 6.3]. It is now proved. [v5, Lem. 4.17, Prop. 6.6]
  • Essential self-adjointness of the smeared generators had gone through analytic vectors of the finite-particle core, [CMP 398, Cor. 4.3], which does not close as stated because the pair-creation terms raise the particle number. It now comes from Nelson’s commutator theorem. [v5, Cor. 4.18]

In the 2022 submitted version of this Letter

  • The 2022 abstract promised “promising results … already with \(128\) logical qubits”, and the introduction “as few as \(N=128\) logical qubits”. 128 sites need 256 logical qubits, because the Jordan–Wigner map uses two qubits per site; and \(N\) is the scale, which is 6, not a qubit count. Both are corrected in the revision. verifiable against Final/qscft_prl_0322_main.tex
  • The 2022 introduction quoted the complexity as \(\delta^{-1}\). It is \(\delta^{-3/2}\): a Heisenberg-limited measurement cost \(\delta^{-1}\) times a discretization cost \(\delta^{-1/2}\) set by the \(1/n^2\) convergence. verifiable against Final/qscft_prl_0322_main.tex

Both of these still stand in the public 2021 preprint. It will be replaced once the human review of this revision is finished; until then this repository is where the corrected statements live.

Resting on a computation

  • The correlator-level convergence of Fig. S7: the two-point function of smeared fields evolved by \(H_k\) converges at \(4^{-N}\) uniformly over the plotted horizon, and at \(k=2\) it grows late in time. This is reproducible from numerics/correlator_convergence.py and is recomputed in your browser by widget H, which reproduces the numpy values to about ten significant figures.
  • The near-term figure: \(\delta_0\approx0.4\) at \(N=6\), \(M=3\), for a D10 wavelet. This number is read off Fig. S2 of the supplement — a one-particle, wavelet-route, \(k=0\) quantity. It is not a correlator bound, and this site does not present it as one.
  • The resource curves of Fig. S6: a calibration of the exponent statement against that one point. Reproducible from numerics/resource_scaling.py; widget F recomputes it.

Open

  • The prefactor \(C_0\) for \(k\neq0\) is not computed. Until it is, the \(k\neq0\) branch of Theorem S1 fixes an exponent and a shape, not a lattice size for a given accuracy. Only \(k=0\) does that.
  • The assembly has not been audited. The companion project was audited adversarially and several of its own repairs turned out to be wrong; this one has had no such pass.
  • Figures S2–S6 have no generating code in the repository. They are the one-particle convergence data from 2021 and arrive as opaque PDFs. Until they are regenerated, the claim that this repository is reproducible end to end is false, and widget J is a pointer rather than a widget.
  • The localization constraint is not met at the near-term point. \(2^M\gtrsim2K-1\) reads \(8\ge19\) at \(M=3\), \(K=10\), and fails. The supplement says so: those observables are wavelet details wrapped around the circle rather than strictly localized fields. The register size is unaffected, but the word “localized” is not earned there.
  • The disposition of the manuscript is not decided.
